Mission Metrics Stamp

Take or pick a photo. The logo goes on. Save it.

How to use

Add a photo

Take a picture or pick one. The logo goes on right away.

On a computer you can also drag photos here or paste them.

Tip: drag the logo on the photo to put it exactly where you want.

Logo style
Pick how the logo looks. Badge is the full round logo. Color is the logo without the white circle. White is an all-white logo that shows up best on dark photos.
See-through
How see-through the logo is. Lower number = more faded into the photo. Around 60% keeps the Mission Metrics colors clear; 30% is a light watermark.
60%
Size
How big the logo is, compared to the photo's shorter side. The logo keeps the same size on every photo, big or small.
20%
Placement
Tap a square to put the logo in that spot on the photo you are looking at. Each photo keeps its own spot, so you can tap the small pictures one by one and choose. Use this spot on all photos copies it to every photo. Repeat fills the whole photo with a pattern of logos, which is harder to crop out. You can also drag the logo on the preview.
4%
Save
Saves at the photo's full original size, as normal picture files (no zip). Save all photos saves every photo, each with the logo where you put it. On a phone, tap Save Images to put them all in Photos. JPG makes smaller files; PNG keeps the most detail.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
