How to stamp a photo

Take a picture → logo goes on → save it. About 20 seconds.

← Back to the app Open MM Stamp

One time only: put it on your home screen

  1. Open the link in Safari

    On your iPhone, open the link above in Safari (the compass icon).

  2. Tap the Share button

    At the bottom of Safari, tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up).

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add in the top right. You now have an MM Stamp icon like a regular app.

Every time: stamp a photo

  1. Open MM Stamp

    Tap the MM Stamp icon on your home screen.

  2. Take the picture

    Tap Take photo. Snap the picture, then tap Use Photo.
    Already have the picture? Tap Choose from photos instead.

  3. Check the logo

    The Mission Metrics logo is now on the bottom-right corner. Want it somewhere else? Drag it with your finger.

  4. Save it

    Tap Save photo, then tap Save Image. The stamped photo is now in your Photos app, ready to post or send.

  5. Next photo

    Tap New photo and repeat.

Several photos at once

  1. Pick several photos

    Tap Choose from photos, tap each picture you want, then tap Add (or Done).

  2. Choose the logo spot on each one

    Tap a small picture under the big photo to open it. Move the logo by dragging it, or tap a square under Placement. Each photo keeps its own spot.
    Want the same spot on all of them? Tap Use this spot on all photos.

  3. Save them all

    Tap Save all photos, then tap Save Images. They go into Photos as normal pictures, not a zip file.

Optional: change the look

Scroll down under the photo:
Logo style — Badge (round logo), Color (no white circle), White (best on dark photos).
See-through — slide to make the logo stronger or lighter.
Size — slide to make it bigger or smaller.
Placement — tap a square to move it to a corner or the middle.
The app remembers your choices for next time.
Android phone? Open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Add to Home screen. Everything else is the same. If you don't see Save Image, tap Download this photo — it goes to your Downloads / Gallery.
Your photos stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. · Mission Metrics
